# Surface brief — world A "Vitrine" (`sites/4d-os/a/index.html`)

**Scope and mode:** the full page of world A (4D.OS desktop + story page). *Experience* mode. Built on the shared engine; behavior follows the specs of the change `add-4d-os-local-demo`.

**Audience and task:** creative technologists who know 4D capture from the reference video. They want to see the whole millipede and move through it. Proof: the synthetic pack running live, with its real figures, always labeled *synthetic*.

**Constraints:** the fixed desktop grammar of the reference aesthetic (old-OS windows, mono pixel type in the chrome, live dithering, museum minimalism, a large neo-grotesque); a cyan/amber/HOLD present; text in English; free fonts; no material from the reference site.

**Memorable moment:** pulling the rail under the vitrine and watching the frustum light sweep across the scene.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The 4D reconstruction is a work on display, not a viewer. The desktop is a painted gallery wall: the live scene hangs in a vitrine, and each OS window is a label, a room plan or the guide device. It refuses the dark viewer with a sci-fi HUD and the light product landing page.

OWN-WORLD: A committed **oxblood red** wall (≈55% of the first screen, the "red room" of a picture gallery). Labels on cold white paper with ink, ink title bars with mono pixel type and tabular inventory numbers (`4D.2026.001`). A vitrine with a 1px frame and crop marks. Alternate rooms on white paper. A night display palette (brick, asphalt, sodium streetlight, moon and the cat) plus oxblood, ink/paper and cyan/amber.

STORY: The visitor walks into "Room 4" and recognizes an exhibition of a single subject in motion. They read the label (title, date, technique and the pack's measured dimensions) and use the rail to travel through time. Further down, each chapter is a room with its wall text.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Oxblood wall, full bleed.
- A centered vitrine, ≈64vw wide at 16:9, with the live scene.
- On the right, in the lower third, the label: "Cat, climbing", *synthetic*, the pack's figures and the inventory number.
- Under the vitrine and as wide as it, the timeline as a museum rail, with the primary action (play).
- Top left the room plan; top right the clock.
- Layers and display folded against the left edge.

Signature interaction: dragging the rail slides the spotlight (the frustum light) across the vitrine.

Elevations the draw contributes:
- *From the painted poster* (declined): "the size is the poster": the vitrine rules and only the title competes with it.
- *From the forge* (declined): the measured state is recorded in tabular numerals.
- *From the isometric pixel-art challenger* (competitive): one hard pixel grid for both render and chrome, with integer scales.

FORM: museum exhibition (vitrine, labels, room plan), position 6 of 7 on the ordered list; direction assigned by the draw; seed `85a92f5b`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
