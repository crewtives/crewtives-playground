# Glossary

The project has its own vocabulary, taken from descriptive geometry, museum practice,
chronophotography and old operating systems. Code, specs, comments and docs all use the terms below,
each with one meaning. Where a term names something in the code, the last column says where to find
it. Paths are relative to the repository root.

Two terms of art stay untranslated: **VISTA** and **épure**. In prose, VISTA is always in capitals
and épure keeps its accent; in identifiers, file names and CSS classes they are written `vista` and
`epure` (`vistaCss`, `.vista__poster`, `epure.ts`, `EpureOptions`). Everything else is plain English.

## Scenes and the 4D pack

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **4D pack** | A scene reconstructed in space and time, stored as files: `scene.json` (metadata), `static.bin` (the background), `dynamic.bin` (the subject, frame by frame) and `source/` (the source frames). It is the contract between whatever produces scenes (today the bake) and the viewer. | Layout in `src/engine/pack/format.ts`; `loader.ts`, `writer.ts`; the packs are in `sites/4d-os/public/packs/`; spec `openspec/specs/4d-pack/spec.md` |
| **Scene** | A subject moving through an environment for a few seconds, seen by a source camera. The published scenes are `cat-stairs` (worlds A, B and C), `falcon-phi` (D) and `whale-fall` (E). | One folder per pack in `sites/4d-os/public/packs/` |
| **Subject** | What moves in a scene: the cat, the falcon or the whale. Its points make up the dynamic layer. | `SceneMeta.counts.dynamic` in `src/engine/pack/format.ts` |
| **Static layer** | The background points, the same in every frame (file magic `4DST`). | `StaticLayer` in `src/engine/pack/format.ts` |
| **Dynamic layer** | The subject's points, sorted by frame (file magic `4DDY`). The points of frame *f* are one contiguous range. | `DynamicLayer` in `src/engine/pack/format.ts` |
| **Frame** | One time step of a scene. A pack has `frameCount` frames at `fps` frames per second. | `SceneMeta` in `src/engine/pack/format.ts` |
| **Source camera** | The camera that "filmed" the scene. The pack records one pose of it per frame. | `PackCamera` in `src/engine/pack/format.ts` |
| **Source frame** | The 2D image the source camera saw at a frame: the "video" the scene is reconstructed from. Packs store source frames as cells of PNG atlas pages. The viewer shows the current one on the frustum's image plane, and windows can show it as a picture. | The `source/` folder of each pack in `sites/4d-os/public/packs/`; `src/engine/shell/sourceFrames.ts`; `SourceLook` in `src/pipeline/bake/recipes/recipe.ts` |
| **Correspondence** | An optional pack property: every frame has the same number of subject points, and point *i* is the same place on the subject in every frame. With it, the viewer can interpolate the present between two frames. The bake produces it from *stable points*. | `SceneMeta.correspondence` in `src/engine/pack/format.ts`; `stableSubject` in `src/pipeline/bake/recipes/recipe.ts` |
| **Synthetic scene** | A scene generated by the bake instead of reconstructed from real footage. Every 4D.OS scene is synthetic, and every page, still and loop that shows one says so. | `SceneMeta.synthetic`; `World.synthetic` in `src/playground/shared/worlds.ts` |
| **Pack weight** | The total size of a pack's files, shown in binary units (KiB, MiB). 4D.OS and the museum print it with the same function. | `packBytes` and `formatBytes` in `src/engine/shell/packStats.ts` |

## Time

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **The NOW** | The current frame, and the only one: the subject's present, the source frame, the frustum, the timecode and the timeline all show it. Each page has a single NOW. In the museum, the NOW is also the dot that marks the current moment on each épure. | `TimeController.frame` in `src/engine/time/TimeController.ts`; spec `openspec/specs/time-viewer/spec.md` |
| **FORWARD, REWIND, HOLD** | The three playback states: time moves forward, moves backward, or stands still. The present takes a different color in each one, set by the tokens `--accent-forward`, `--accent-rewind` and `--accent-hold` (cyan, amber and near-white by default). | `play`, `rewind`, `hold` in `src/engine/time/TimeController.ts`; `ClockState` in `src/playground/museum/clock.ts` |
| **Speed steps** | The multiples of real time that J and L step through (1, 2, 4 and 8 times). The museum's page clock has no speeds. | `DEFAULT_SPEED_STEPS` in `src/engine/time/TimeController.ts`; `src/engine/shell/keyboard.ts` |
| **Memory mode, all-at-once mode** | The two time modes. Memory shows the present and the past, never the future. All at once also shows the future, dimmed. | `TimeMode` in `src/engine/time/TimeController.ts` |
| **Present** | The subject at the NOW, drawn in the color of the playback state. With correspondence it is interpolated between frames, so it moves smoothly at any speed. | `setPresentLook` in `src/engine/viewer/TimeViewer.ts` |
| **Trail** | The subject's other moments, drawn together with the present and fading with their distance in time from it. Showing one frame in every *k* (the trail stride) turns the trail into distinct copies. In the museum, a work's trail is the path its épure draws. | `ViewerLayers.trail`, `setTrailStride` and `setTrailLook` in `src/engine/viewer/TimeViewer.ts`; `src/playground/museum/build/trail.ts` |
| **Millipede** | The informal name for a trail that reads as a row of copies of the subject side by side, like a chronophotograph by Étienne-Jules Marey. World B, the Plate, is built around it. | `src/engine/shell/sidePreset.ts`; `src/4d-os/worlds/b/` |
| **Timecode** | The NOW written as a video timecode. | `timecode` in `src/engine/time/TimeController.ts` |
| **Segment** | A stretch of time or of travel with a behavior of its own: the zoom and final segments of the hero gesture, the loop segment of an épure, each twist of the column on sheet 000. | `HeroPhases` in `src/engine/shell/zoomHero.ts`; `Trail.span` in `src/playground/museum/build/manifest.ts`; `Segment` in `src/playground/museum/build/method.ts` |

## Drawing the scene

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **Engine** | One full-screen canvas and one WebGL renderer per page. It paints every view into the rectangle of its DOM element and renders only when something asks it to. | `src/engine/engine/Engine.ts` |
| **View** | Anything the engine paints: a DOM element plus a `render` function. A page can have many views and still has one WebGL context. | `EngineView` in `src/engine/engine/Engine.ts` |
| **Viewer** | The view that draws a 4D pack in time: the present, the trail, the background, the source camera's frustum and its trajectory, with the camera controls. | `src/engine/viewer/TimeViewer.ts` |
| **Frustum, frustum light** | The source camera's viewing pyramid, drawn in the scene with the current source frame on its image plane. Background points inside it are lit; those outside are dimmed. | `ViewerLayers.frustum` in `src/engine/viewer/TimeViewer.ts`; `src/engine/viewer/packGpu.ts` |
| **Trajectory** | The line through the source camera's positions over the whole scene. | `ViewerLayers.trajectory` in `src/engine/viewer/TimeViewer.ts` |
| **Dollhouse cutaway** | Hiding the background points that stand between the camera and the subject, like the missing front wall of a dollhouse. | `setCutaway` in `src/engine/viewer/TimeViewer.ts`; `src/engine/viewer/static.vert.glsl` |
| **Retro display** | The pass that gives every scene the look of an old monitor: the scene is rendered at low resolution (a third, by default) and quantized to a small palette. Its color depth is one of three modes: 1-bit, 16 colors or Millions. | `src/engine/display/RetroDisplay.ts`, `src/engine/display/display.frag.glsl`; spec `openspec/specs/dither-display/spec.md` |
| **Dither** | The ordered (Bayer) pattern the retro display uses to approach colors that are not in its palette. It is an 8×8 threshold matrix, also used for the dissolve that reveals a view. | `bayer8` in `src/engine/display/display.frag.glsl` |
| **Block** | One pixel of the retro display: a square of B×B device pixels, aligned from the bottom-left corner of its view. | `blockSize` in `src/engine/display/RetroDisplay.ts`; `src/playground/museum/loops/blocks.ts` |
| **Palette tokens** | The CSS custom properties that set each world's 1-bit and 16-color palettes (`--pal-1bit-*`, `--pal-16-*`). JavaScript reads them from CSS, so the design system and the display always agree. | `readPalettes` in `src/engine/display/palette.ts`; each world's `tokens.css` |
| **Pinch** | Zoom that belongs to a view: touch, trackpad or Safari gestures zoom the scene, while the mouse wheel keeps scrolling the page. | `src/engine/viewer/pinch.ts` |
| **Chase camera** | A third-person camera that follows the subject along its path. | `src/engine/shell/chaseCam.ts` |
| **Close-up** | A framing of the subject at a single frame, at an angle to the source camera's axis and slightly raised, so a small subject in a large scene is not lost. | `src/engine/shell/closeUp.ts` |
| **Side preset** | A fixed camera that looks at the subject's path from the side, with a narrow field of view, so the trail reads as a flat plate. | `src/engine/shell/sidePreset.ts` |

## 4D.OS

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **4D.OS** | The site at `/4d-os/`: a launcher and five worlds that share one engine, styled as the desktop of an old operating system. | `sites/4d-os/`, `src/4d-os/` |
| **World** | One of the five 4D.OS pages, A to E, each a visual language of its own over the same engine: A Vitrine, B Plate and C Leader show the cat; D The golden stoop shows the falcon; E Whale fall shows the whale. The code and the UI both call them worlds. | `src/4d-os/worlds/<letter>/`; `data-world` attributes; the registry `WORLDS` in `src/playground/shared/worlds.ts` |

| **Desktop** | The first screen of a world as an old OS desktop: the viewer is the wallpaper, and windows are the tools for time, camera, layers and display. | `src/engine/shell/desktop.ts`; spec `openspec/specs/desktop-shell/spec.md` |
| **Window** | A draggable DOM window on the desktop. The engine provides the behavior; each world provides the styles. | `src/engine/window/windows.ts` |
| **Window trail** | The echo a window leaves while it is dragged: copies at its earlier positions, cleared on release. It is the desktop's 2D version of the scene's trail. | `src/engine/shell/windowTrail.ts` |
| **Boot** | The boot window that shows real load progress, followed by the dissolve that reveals the scene. | `src/engine/shell/boot.ts` |
| **Timeline** | The track that shows the NOW and lets the visitor move it. | `src/engine/shell/timeline.ts` |
| **Rail** | The track of a timeline or scrubber, along which the playhead moves. In world A it names the timeline window itself; in Wind-Up Empire, the rocket's launch rail. | `.rail` in `sites/4d-os/a/index.html`; `.clock__rail` in `src/playground/museum/build/render.ts` |
| **Room, label** | World A presents the scene as an exhibition. Its sections are numbered rooms, and the window that describes the scene is its label, as in a gallery. | `sites/4d-os/a/index.html`, `src/4d-os/worlds/a/` |
| **Wall clock** | The real date and time in the Clock window, as distinct from the scene's timecode. | `src/engine/shell/wallClock.ts` |
| **Hero, hero gesture** | The first screen stays pinned while scrolling, pinching or the keyboard zoom into the scene. Time loops meanwhile, and a final segment carries it to the last frame before the next section appears. | `src/engine/shell/zoomHero.ts`; spec `openspec/specs/hero-gesture/spec.md` |
| **Story page** | The chapters below a world's first screen. In worlds A, B and C they explain 4D reconstruction (2D plus time, the pipeline, the figures), and in worlds A and C scrolling drives the scene's time; D and E tell the story of their own scenes. | `src/engine/shell/scrollTime.ts`, `src/engine/shell/smoothScroll.ts`; specs `openspec/specs/story-page/spec.md` (A, B and C) and `openspec/specs/cosmic-landings/spec.md` (D and E) |
| **Cosmic landings** | The name under which worlds D and E were specified. They are 4D.OS worlds, not playground landings. | Spec `openspec/specs/cosmic-landings/spec.md` |

## The bake

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **Bake** | Producing a 4D pack in the browser, in development, from a recipe. Each frame, the baker poses the subject, samples points on it and renders the source frame, then writes the pack files. Baking is deterministic on the same browser and GPU: the same recipe and parameters give the same bytes. The source frames are GPU renders encoded as PNG by the browser, so another machine can produce different PNG bytes. | `sites/4d-os/bake.html`, `src/pipeline/bake/main.ts`, `src/pipeline/bake/SyntheticScene.ts`; spec `openspec/specs/synthetic-bake/spec.md` |
| **Recipe** | Everything specific to one scene: the subject and its motion, the environment, the lights, the source camera and the look of the source frames. `/bake.html?scene=<recipe>` picks one. | `Recipe` in `src/pipeline/bake/recipes/recipe.ts`; `RECIPES` in `src/pipeline/bake/recipes/index.ts` |
| **Scene equations** | The pure math of the computed scenes: the falcon's golden spiral and the whale's fall into a black hole. The baker, worlds D and E, and the museum's captions all use them, so every figure agrees. | `src/pipeline/scenes/` |
| **Procedural subject** | A subject built from equations instead of a 3D model: the falcon and the whale. | `src/pipeline/bake/recipes/falconPhiBody.ts`, `whaleFallBody.ts`; spec `openspec/specs/procedural-subject/spec.md` |
| **Stable points** | Subject points chosen once, on the pose of frame 0, and kept on the same place of the body in every frame. They give the pack its correspondence. | `stableSubject` in `src/pipeline/bake/recipes/recipe.ts` |
| **Seed (random)** | The integer that starts the seeded random generator. The bake never uses `Math.random`, so a recipe always produces the same scene. | `src/pipeline/scenes/random.ts` |
| **Cat motion** | The cat's walk is built from planned footfalls, two-bone inverse kinematics, a spine built in code and springs. The model is the CC-BY 3.0 "Cat" by J-Toastie (see `LICENSES.md`). | `src/pipeline/bake/recipes/catGait.ts`, `catIk.ts`, `catSpine.ts`, `catSprings.ts`; the model in `src/pipeline/models/cat/` |

## The museum

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **Museum** | The page at `/`: a set of technical sheets, one per work, under a single page clock. It is generated at build time and is complete without JavaScript. | `sites/playground/index.html`, `src/playground/museum/`; spec `openspec/specs/playground-museum/spec.md` |
| **The house** | The museum's own identity (paper, ink, grid, typefaces, palette), as opposed to the works' identities. "House pixels" and "the house method" belong to it. | `src/playground/museum/tokens.css` |
| **Work** | A piece in the museum's collection. There are four: The cat, The golden stoop, Whale fall and Bloomscope. One work can appear in several worlds: The cat is shown in A, B and C. | `WorkSheet` and `SHEETS` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Collection** | The hand-curated list of works, and the only hand-written data about the works: numbers, titles, dates, wall texts, routes and source paths. Every other figure is read from the work itself. | `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Sheet** | A technical sheet for one work: its VISTA, its épure with the NOW, its title block, its wall text and the link "Enter \<title\>". Each sheet has a three-digit number and the anchor `#sheet-NNN`. The house's paper color is also called the sheet (`--sheet`). | `sheetArticle` in `src/playground/museum/build/render.ts` |
| **Sheet number** | Three digits. A number is never reused, even after its sheet is retired, and 000 is reserved for the house. | `RETIRED_NUMBERS` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Featured sheet** | The sheet on the first screen: the one pinned in the collection, or else the one with the highest number. | `FEATURED`, `featuredSheet` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Method sheet (sheet 000)** | The house method: Gaudí's double-twist column and the tesseract, drawn as épures, to teach how to read a sheet. Its height is height, not time. | `METHOD_SHEET` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts`; `src/playground/museum/build/method.ts` |
| **VISTA** | The framed view of a work on its sheet: a loop recorded from the work's live render, shown pixel-exact at an integer scale inside a passe-partout. It is the largest region of every sheet. | `vista` in `src/playground/museum/build/render.ts`; `src/playground/museum/player.ts` |
| **Passe-partout** (code: `mat`) | The frame around a VISTA, in the work's own background color (not the house's paper), read from the work's CSS tokens, with an ink rule. | `SheetView.mat` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts`; `readToken` in `src/playground/museum/build/manifest.ts` |
| **Title block** | The sheet's data, as in the corner of an engineering drawing, ending with the link "Enter \<title\>". The number, the title, the "synthetic" mark and the stale-loop notice are always visible; the series, form, date, technique, dimensions with time, weight, rule and provenance line are in a disclosure labeled "Sheet data". | `titleBlock` in `src/playground/museum/build/render.ts`; `.title-block` in `src/playground/museum/style.css` |
| **Wall text** | The short text beside a work, as on a museum wall: 80 words at most, with no hand-written figures. | `WorkSheet.text` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Series** | A group of works; today only the 4D.OS series (sheets 001 to 003). | `WorkSheet.series` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Form** | The kind of work, drawn as a mark: ○ a scene with a 4D pack, □ a toy, △ a workshop sheet. | `Form`, `FORM_NAME` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts`; `formMark` in `src/playground/museum/build/render.ts` |
| **Toy** | Something you play with rather than watch: Bloomscope in the collection, and the machines and gadgets of the landings. | `Form` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Rule** | The equation behind a work, written in the title block and built from the same constants its code uses. | `Rule` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Sheet index** | The museum's second section: one row per work sheet, with number, form, poster, title, line, dimensions and date, and a separator line whenever the date changes. | `indexSection` in `src/playground/museum/build/render.ts` |
| **Gate row** | The unnumbered index row just before the 4D.OS series, linking to the launcher: "4D.OS — Five worlds, one launcher. Every moment of a scene, all at once." | `GATE_ROW` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Workshop sheets** | Three placeholder rows at the end of the index, marked △ and "Being drawn. Not public yet.", with no number, name, link or date. | `WORKSHOP_SHEETS`, `WORKSHOP_TEXT` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Colophon** | The museum's closing section: how the page is made, its typefaces, code and models with their licenses, what it stores, the references with their sources, and a glossary. | `colophon` in `src/playground/museum/build/render.ts`; `src/playground/museum/references.ts` |
| **Page clock** | The single clock of the museum: it drives every loop and every NOW on the page together. It has three states (FORWARD, REWIND, HOLD) and no speeds. | `PageClock` in `src/playground/museum/clock.ts` |
| **Scrubber** | The control that moves the page clock by hand. It chases its target a few frames at a time and does not flip direction too quickly, so scrubbing never flashes. | `ScrubChase` in `src/playground/museum/clock.ts` |
| **Wash** | The page's light: soft lavender patches at the top (morning) and apricot at the bottom (afternoon). Its intensity depends only on the scroll position. | `.light` in `src/playground/museum/style.css`; `paintLight` in `src/playground/museum/main.ts`; `src/playground/museum/tokens.css` |
| **Grid** | The house's modular grid, visible in the margins and rules; G or the Grid button shows it over the whole page. | `src/playground/museum/style.css`; `toggleGrid` in `src/playground/museum/main.ts` |
| **House pixels** | The museum's color-depth selector (1-bit, 16, Millions). It affects only an open fold, never the loops or the épures. | `src/playground/museum/fold.ts` |
| **Manifest** | Everything the build reads for each work: the curation, the pack's figures and trail, the loops' provenance and the stale-loop check. | `buildManifest` in `src/playground/museum/build/manifest.ts` |
| **Figures read from the work** | The honesty rule of the museum: every figure, date and stroke about a work comes from its pack, its code, its loop or the collection, and an automatic check marks the source of each figure on the page. | `src/playground/museum/build/figures.ts` |

## The épure

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **The épure (design direction)** | The name of the museum's design direction: the museum as a descriptive-geometry drawing in a daylit room, with ink on the sheet. The specs and the archived changes use its working name, "La épura". | `docs/design/DESIGN.md` ("Playground: the museum 'The épure'"); `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Épure** | A drawing from Gaspard Monge's descriptive geometry: an object shown in plan and in elevation on one sheet, joined by the ground line, as if the vertical plane had been folded flat onto the horizontal one. On each sheet, the épure draws the work's trail: the plan says where, the elevation says when. | `src/playground/museum/build/epure.ts`; the visitor-facing definition is `GLOSSARY` in `src/playground/museum/references.ts` |
| **Plan** | The lower view of an épure, seen from above. On a work sheet it shows where each moment happened. | `src/playground/museum/build/epure.ts` |
| **Elevation** | The upper view of an épure, seen from the front. On a work sheet, height is time: each moment sits higher than the one before. | `src/playground/museum/build/epure.ts` |
| **Ground line** | The line between plan and elevation, where the two planes meet. It is also the hinge the fold turns on. | `src/playground/museum/build/epure.ts` |
| **Reference line** | The line, perpendicular to the ground line, that joins the NOW in the elevation with the NOW in the plan. | `src/playground/museum/build/epure.ts` |
| **Loop segment** | The part of the trail that the work's loop covers, drawn solid over the dotted full trail. | `Trail.span` in `src/playground/museum/build/manifest.ts` |
| **Fold** | The museum's only 3D moment: the épure's vertical plane turns about the ground line from 0° (flat, as printed) to 90°, and the trail rises into space. Without WebGL2, an axonometric SVG made at build time stands in for it. | `src/playground/museum/fold.ts`, `fold3d.ts`; `src/playground/museum/build/axonometry.ts`, `geom3.ts` |
| **Dihedron** | The two planes of an épure at 90° to each other, as the fold shows them. | `src/playground/museum/build/geom3.ts` |

## Loops and provenance

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **Loop** | A short, deterministic recording of a work's live render (45 frames at 15 fps), pixel-exact to its display, played in the work's VISTA. | `src/playground/museum/loops/`; files in `sites/playground/public/loops/<id>/`; spec `openspec/specs/work-loops/spec.md` |
| **Pass** | One direction of a loop: the FORWARD pass and the REWIND pass, each a gzip-compressed 4DLP file. Bloomscope only has a FORWARD pass, because it cannot rewind. | `passesFor`, `LoopPass` in `src/playground/museum/loops/provenance.ts` |
| **4DLP** | The loop file format: 4-bit palette indices, whole frames, rows interleaved across frames. | `src/playground/museum/loops/format.ts` |
| **Poster** | Frame 0 of the FORWARD pass as a lossless WebP. It is what the VISTA shows before the loop loads and when there is no JavaScript. | `poster.webp` in each loop folder |
| **Poster sidecar** | `poster.webp.json`, next to the poster, with its provenance line and hash. The capture tool writes it. | `posterSidecar` in `src/playground/museum/loops/provenance.ts` |
| **Provenance** | The published record of how a loop was made: the work, the route, the capture settings, the palette, the date, the commit it was recorded at, the sources hash, the tool and browser, and a hash of every frame. The title block summarizes it in one line. | `LoopProvenance`, `validateProvenance` in `src/playground/museum/loops/provenance.ts`; `provenance.json` in each loop folder |
| **Sources** | The files whose changes make a work's loop stale: its pack, its page and its code, plus the engine and the 4D.OS launcher, which `CORE` adds to every sheet. Each work lists them in the collection. | `WorkSheet.sources` and `CORE` in `src/playground/museum/collection.ts` |
| **Sources hash** | A SHA-256 over every file under a work's sources, in sorted path order, skipping dot-files, with each file's path, length and bytes. The provenance records it at recording time; the build recomputes it. It needs no git history. | `sourcesHash` in `src/playground/museum/build/sources.ts` |
| **Stale loop** | A loop whose recorded sources hash no longer matches the work. The build warns without failing, and the title block says "recorded \<date\>; the work has changed since". | `LoopEntry.stale` in `src/playground/museum/build/manifest.ts` |
| **Frame hash** | A SHA-256 over a native loop frame in 8-bit RGBA. It uses WebCrypto, so the capture tool and the museum's own code compute it the same way in Node and in the browser. | `frameHash` in `src/playground/museum/loops/hash.ts` |
| **Capture tool** | The development tool that records the loops from the built site in a browser, under a controlled clock, and verifies them. | `tools/capture-loops.ts`; per-loop settings in `src/playground/museum/capture.ts` |

## Bloomscope

## The other landings

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **Landing** | A story page of the playground: Bloomscope, Game Center Yonjigen and Wind-Up Empire. They began as candidates for the front page. Bloomscope became a work; the other two stay outside the collection at `/landings/<slug>/`. | `src/playground/`; spec `openspec/specs/playground-hub/spec.md` |
| **Game Center Yonjigen** | A Tokyo arcade building: scrolling is the elevator, each floor is a genre, and each machine is playable. 1F Rain Run, 2F the 4D.OS index, 3F the Win a World claw machine, 4F the Parlour Glass, 5F the lab, and the roof with the Gas Tuner and the service panel. | `src/playground/game-center/`, one folder per machine (`rainrun`, `crane`, `parlour`, `gas`, `service`, `roof`); spec `openspec/specs/landing-game-center/spec.md` |
| **Machine** | A Game Center toy. Most machines (crane, rainrun, parlour) are split into a pure simulation (`sim`), its drawing (`view`), its DOM (`machine`) and its startup (`boot`). | For example `src/playground/game-center/crane/` |
| **Wind-Up Empire** | A space empire as a lithographed tin toy you wind up, with a declared-fake demo economy, five physical toys (friction rocket, planet tops, wind-up key, litho press, spark wheel) and the five worlds as planets of a tin orrery. | `src/playground/wind-up-empire/`; spec `openspec/specs/landing-wind-up-empire/spec.md` |
| **Still** | A lossless WebP capture of a world's live render, used by the landings; each one has its provenance. | `sites/playground/public/landings/_shared/stills/`; the A–C source captures in `src/pipeline/captures/`, while D and E come from `sites/4d-os/public/launcher/`; `World.still` in `src/playground/shared/worlds.ts` |

## Across the project

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
|---|---|---|
| **Playground** | The site at `/` that holds the museum, Bloomscope and the two other landings. | `sites/playground/`, `src/playground/` |
| **Visitor** | The person using a page. Specs say "the visitor", never "the user". | `openspec/specs/` |
| **Demo index** | The list of the five worlds and the launcher that every landing shows in its own style, from one registry, so routes, stills, lines and credits are never written twice. | `WORLDS`, `LAUNCHER` in `src/playground/shared/worlds.ts` |
| **Cat credit** | The required CC-BY attribution of the cat model, shown next to every image or loop of the cat on the site. The share images that show the cat carry it in their metadata (alt text, structured data, sidecar and `provenance.json`), not in the image. | `CAT_CREDIT` in `src/playground/shared/worlds.ts`; `LICENSES.md` |
| **Build stamp** | The version label the playground pages show ("demo build 0.1"). | `BUILD_STAMP` in `src/playground/shared/worlds.ts` |
| **Reduced motion** | The visitor's `prefers-reduced-motion` setting, followed live without a reload. Nothing moves on its own: the page clock starts in HOLD, the wash stays fixed, and a toy jumps to its result instead of animating the path. | `src/playground/shared/motion.ts` |
| **Design brief** | The written direction for one page's design, kept for the design tooling. The design system they build on is `docs/design/DESIGN.md`. | `.impeccable/surfaces/` |
| **Decision (Dn)** | A numbered decision in the design of an OpenSpec change. Each change numbers its own, so a "D4" in a comment belongs to the change that shaped that module; [`architecture.md`](architecture.md#decision-references-in-comments) lists which is which. | `openspec/changes/archive/*/design.md` |
