# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Believable subject motion
The motion of the bake's animated subjects SHALL be believable. It is measurable in the bake, at 120 samples per second:
- **footfalls:** a planted foot MUST NOT slide more than 1 cm during its stance, nor sink more than 5 mm into the surface it steps on;
- **continuity (no pose cuts):** measured with 1 ms differences across the whole clip, no vertex SHALL exceed 40 m/s and no bone 40 rad/s. A fast leg or wing stays well below that; a pose cut, which is a jump in 0 ms, lands well above it;
- **variation:** repeated steps, jumps or wingbeats SHALL vary deterministically with the seed, and their durations SHALL have a coefficient of variation between 3 % and 15 %;
- **rest:** at rest (sitting, perched), the subject SHALL rest on its body and its legs, not on its tail or its head.

#### Scenario: Planted foot
- **WHEN** the world position of each of the cat's feet is measured during each of its stances on the staircase
- **THEN** no foot slides more than 1 cm or ends up more than 5 mm below its footprint

#### Scenario: No pose cuts
- **WHEN** the subject's pose is evaluated at every sample (120 per second) and 1 ms later, across the whole clip
- **THEN** no vertex moves faster than 40 m/s and no bone rotates faster than 40 rad/s

#### Scenario: Sitting on its haunches
- **WHEN** the cat ends up sitting on the stair landing
- **THEN** its haunches and its four paws are less than 1 cm from the landing and the tail is not the point of support

### Requirement: Stable subject points
A recipe SHALL be able to request that the subject's points be chosen once on its surface and reused in every frame. In that case, point `i` in every frame is the same place on the body, and the pack declares correspondence between frames (see `4d-pack`). Recipes that do not request it MUST NOT change their bytes.

#### Scenario: Baked correspondence
- **WHEN** a recipe with stable points is baked
- **THEN** every frame has the same number of points and the median displacement of a point between consecutive frames is less than twice the displacement of the subject's center

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Marking and licensing of synthetic content
Every pack generated by the bake SHALL declare `synthetic: true`. The bake MUST use only freely licensed models: CC0 or public domain, or CC-BY. A CC-BY model MUST be credited (author, license and source) in `LICENSES.md` and in a visible credit on every page that shows it. A subject generated by original equations, with no third-party model, does not require third-party credit. Even so, the page SHALL state that the subject is synthetic and computed.

#### Scenario: Synthetic flag
- **WHEN** the metadata of a pack generated by the bake is inspected
- **THEN** it declares `synthetic: true`

#### Scenario: Model with attribution
- **WHEN** the pack uses a CC-BY model
- **THEN** `LICENSES.md` names the author, license and source, and every page that shows the scene includes that credit in visible text

#### Scenario: Computed subject
- **WHEN** the pack uses a subject generated by original equations
- **THEN** the page that shows it states in visible text that it is synthetic and computed, and `LICENSES.md` records it as original work
