The Scope, live

The one angle no mirror can make.

GOLDEN

Turn the outer dial. Hold Shift, or use the thin inner ring, for thousandths of a degree.Turn the outer dial with a finger; the thin inner ring moves it by thousandths of a degree.

Mirrors only close into a pattern at 180°/n; the golden angle is not one of them. A sunflower sets each new seed 137.508° past the last, so no two seeds ever line up on a spoke. Every seed stays where it was born, so the head is a picture of its own past. Nudge the dial off the golden angle and watch it break.

The Scope, live

Honeycomb keeps every generation.

Paint cells, press Run. It is a hexagonal cousin of Life (rule B2/S34), and it keeps each generation as a layer of wax. Cells that last six generations get capped.

generation 31
Pixels

Keyboard: arrows move the cursor along the comb (Shift+Up and Shift+Down take the other diagonal), Enter paints or erases, R runs, S steps, B rewinds. Put in the Scope takes the 37 cells around the cursor.
