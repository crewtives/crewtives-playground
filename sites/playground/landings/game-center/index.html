Rain Run · How to play

Fly the taxi through the lit gates. Your flight stays on screen; press C, or crash three times, and the whole flight turns sideways, every moment at once.

Steer
Drag the stick, or arrow keys / WASD
A · Boost
Z key. 1.5× speed for 1.2 s, then 3 s to recharge
B · Ghost
X key. Your best run, flying beside you
C · Time view
C key. Pauses and shows the whole flight
Start
Enter, the 1P START button, or tap the screen

90 seconds, 3 lives. Each gate scores 100 × your combo, up to ×8; a missed gate resets it. HI is kept in this browser only.

Win a World · How to play

Each capsule holds a 4D.OS world or a studio sticker. Two buttons, one try, like the real machines. The claw grips 80% of the time. The only prizes are links, and all of them are listed below.

Button 1
Hold to move the claw right. Right arrow or 1
Button 2
Hold to move it back; let go and it drops. Up arrow or 2
One try
Each button works once per try, then both light up again

A near miss can still nudge a capsule over the lip of the prize chute.

Win a world

Behind the glass, capsules sit in one layer on a carpet floor, each marked with a world letter, 4D for the launcher, or a sticker rosette. The prize chute is at the front left. Synthetic scene.

    Parlour Glass · How to play

    Turn the handle, pour, and leave the shutter open: every ball's path stays on the glass. Spin the wheel back and they all climb home.

    Handle
    Drag, or arrow keys: steps of 5, Shift for 20
    Launch
    Hold the button, or hold Space on it
    Wheel
    Turn the rim to step back 1/30 s at a time; twist the inner ring to shuttle. J rewinds, K holds, L plays
    Now
    Back to the present

    Free balls. No bets, no prizes, nothing saved.
