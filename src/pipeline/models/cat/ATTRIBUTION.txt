Cat.glb in this folder is the original file, unmodified.

Modifications in the 4D packs baked from it (recipes in src/pipeline/bake/recipes/):
- fur darkened to a black cat: albedo x 0.14, except the eyes;
- procedural animation (planned footsteps, leg IK, a spine added in code, springs),
  applied at bake time without changing the model file.
